Title: And It Might Not Be True️

Year: 2025

Medium: multimedia projection

Exhibited at: Layer Gallery, Bucharest-Romania, 2025

This work reflects on misinformation and the nature of truth. To understand what is going wrong, and to trace where misinformation begins, we must be critical not only of others but, most importantly, of ourselves. We can easily fall into thinking traps like cherry picking or believe biases, but it is our responsibility to know that.
