Title: Fountain for Golfers

Year: 2024

Medium: PVC, metal, synthetic grass, water pump

Exhibited at: Aqua Invísivel, Lagos-Portugal, 2024 | Museum of Contemporary art of Tuvalu, Agueda-Portugal, 2025

In Lagos (Portugal), the beauty of the vibrant green golf courses stands in stark contrast to the arid land around them. While working on the theme of water I remembered this sentence: “the grass is greener where you water it”. Meaning, the things you put most attention to will grow the most. This installation is questioning whether that grass should be the one of the golf courts.
