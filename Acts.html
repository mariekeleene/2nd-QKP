Title: Acts Of Belonging

Year: 2026

Medium: Multimedia installation

Showed at: S+T+ARTS Aqua Motion residency

As the boundaries to protect territories are regularly being redrawn, both physically and conceptually, what do our individual actions towards these spaces say of our sense of belonging to them? in What ways do these actions have a direct effect on our own sense of wellbeing and on the wellbeing of the spaces themselves? Through an immersive and interactive installation representing the landscape of the Litoral Norte Natural Park, participants are invited to critically explore duality between the anthropogenic impact on the environment and the human sense of belonging to nature.
