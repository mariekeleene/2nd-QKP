Title: Lets Keep It Gezellig

Year: 2022

Medium: multimedia installation

Showed at: ArtEZ Finals Arnhem, Netherlands, The Vibe of the Future Festival Nijmegen, Netherlands, AgitLAB, Portugal

There are times when we feel overwhelmed by all around disaster: the bio-industry’s large-scale slaughtering of animals, the pandemic, wars, nuclear threat, climate change, natural disasters - to name just a few. The awareness of the pain of the world can be paralyzing. How can you escape these facts and continue to live your life in peace without falling into ignorance. I have tried to capture this contradictory feeling in 40 light-hearted animations that show melting icecaps, broken appliances, an endless stream of packages, flight shame, a pile of dead chickens, a tea cup pouring over, a horse pulling a dead horse, and me myself - drowning, ploughing trough thick snow, over and over again.
